PhishermanAI settings

You control where your messages are checked.

Defaults to your own machine. Point this at your own server if you host the verifier elsewhere — the extension will only ever contact this address, and it is not permitted to reach any other host.

Consent

Checking stays off until you confirm it in the panel the first time you use it. You can withdraw consent at any time from the toolbar popup; the extension then stops sending anything.

What is sent

Only the text of the single message you click "Check this" on, with phone numbers and email addresses stripped out locally first. UPI payment addresses are kept, because where the money is going is the main thing being checked. Nothing else leaves your browser.
