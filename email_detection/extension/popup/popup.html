How to use it

  1. Open web.whatsapp.com.
  2. Hover the suspicious message.
  3. Click Check this.

What this extension does not do

  • It never sends, replies to, or forwards messages for you.
  • It reads only the one message you click on — never your chats, contacts, or phone number.
  • Phone numbers and email addresses are removed before anything is sent.
  • It talks only to the endpoint above, which is your own machine by default.
  • No message content is stored, by the extension or the verifier.
