## pHash drift under WhatsApp recompression

Measured, not assumed. This is what justifies the matcher's distance thresholds, and it quantifies the fragility that metadata-based provenance (C2PA and similar) suffers on this channel — a signed manifest does not survive a single re-encode, whereas a perceptual hash drifts only slightly.

| recompression generations | mean distance | max | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1x | 2.2 | 6 | 10 |
| 3x | 3.6 | 6 | 10 |
| 5x | 4.8 | 8 | 10 |
| **different documents** | **52.93** | — | 45 |

> Same document after 5 re-forwards stays within 8 bits, while unrelated documents are never closer than 22 bits. The two populations do not overlap, so the < 10 threshold for "same document" is supported by the data rather than chosen arbitrarily.
