# Precision hardening — measured evidence

Generated by `python -m eval.report_hardening`. Companion to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md), which covers the fixture-level metrics.

## What is being measured

A golden corpus of **155 genuine communications** that must never be flagged, of which **35 are adversarial** — real investor-awareness material from exchanges, the regulator and brokers whose *subject matter is fraud*. Those are the hardest possible inputs for a rule engine: they contain every phrase a detector looks for, used to warn against it.

Two precision mechanisms are ablated independently:

- **Authorised-sender short-circuit** — a direct email with a valid, *aligned* DKIM signature from a domain we hold positive evidence for returns GENUINE without running content rules at all. DKIM already proves the content is unmodified, so re-checking it can only invent false positives.
- **Awareness suppression** — a document-level heuristic recognising material that *describes* fraud while naming no payment destination and requesting no credential.

## Ablation

| configuration | FP | FP rate | adversarial FP | short-circuit | fraud recall | p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| both enabled (shipped) | 0 | **0.0%** | 0 | 80.6% | 95.0% | 10 ms |
| awareness suppression OFF | 3 | **1.9%** | 3 | 80.6% | 95.0% | 13 ms |
| authorised-sender short-circuit OFF | 0 | **0.0%** | 0 | 0.0% | 95.0% | 43 ms |
| both OFF (contextual rules alone) | 6 | **3.9%** | 6 | 0.0% | 95.0% | 36 ms |

### Reading the table

- **Precision cost nothing in recall.** Fraud recall is 95.0% in every configuration: both mechanisms remove false positives without removing a single detection. That is the result worth reporting — precision and recall are usually a trade, and here they were not.
- **Every false positive under rules alone is adversarial material.** 6 of 6 FPs come from the 35-sample adversarial set (17% of it), and none from the other 120 samples. Investor-awareness copy is precisely where a keyword system fails.
- **The two mechanisms are complementary, not redundant.** The short-circuit handles adversarial mail arriving *directly* from an authorised domain; awareness suppression handles the same content *forwarded*, where the original signature is destroyed and no short-circuit is possible.
- **Latency.** The short-circuit answers in 10 ms against 36 ms for the full pipeline, on 81% of genuine mail.

## Evasion resistance

A fraudster could try to disarm the claim rules by pasting awareness vocabulary into a pitch. Awareness suppression is therefore disqualified by the presence of any payment destination or credential request, and the MONEY and DELIVERY chokepoints are never suppressed by it.

| probe | caught |
|---|---|
| beware + personal UPI | yes |
| advisory + bank account | yes |
| ponzi word + OTP request | yes |

> All evasion probes caught. Money remains the arbiter: the moment a message names somewhere to send money, awareness suppression stops applying.

## Limitations

- Awareness suppression is a **document-level heuristic**, not semantic understanding. It is a deliberately conservative stand-in for an LLM adjudicator (Phase 9 Part C, not built), which would judge the ROLE a flagged phrase plays — PROMISING versus WARNING — rather than inferring it from document-level markers. The adjudicator OFF/ON comparison the brief asks for therefore **cannot be reported**: there is no adjudicator to switch on. The table above ablates what exists.
- The corpus is real-*shaped* but synthetic. It follows the structure and wording of genuine notices; it is not a sample of real inboxes. Substituting real `.eml` files would strengthen every number here.
- The fraud set is 20 hand-written samples covering the rule taxonomy, not a field-collected corpus, so the recall figure describes coverage of known patterns rather than performance against live campaigns.

## Reproducing

```bash
python -m eval.make_golden          # 120 genuine institutional samples
python -m eval.make_adversarial     # 35 adversarial (subject matter IS fraud)
python -m eval.run_golden           # assert zero false positives
python -m eval.report_hardening     # regenerate this ablation
```
